# Accepted mechanics semantic audit

This audit distinguishes compiled operations from checked semantics. It is based on the pinned game-data source and direct production interpreter inspection, not merely finite output or raw/prepared parity. Counts below are operation occurrences in the 341-character audit snapshot; duplicate linked branches occur and counts are not unique character counts.

## Other accepted approximations requiring review

- Source `resultVarianceDecimal` is ignored in 918 operation occurrences. A symmetric linear damage variance can preserve mean damage, but crit caps, current-pool saturation, lethal constraints and nonlinear U may make mean-only treatment approximate. This is not automatically a defect in a mean-field model; document the mean assumption and do not claim exact battle outcome coverage.
- `maxBonusMoves` appears above one in 53 accepted occurrences, frequently six for mass assists. It is a bonus-action scheduler bound, not evidence of a generic buff-stack cap. Do not infer global nonstacking from its name.
- Stat buffs are additive expected changes from original stats, with multiplier bounded to [-1,3]. Source stacking/refresh/remove order is not represented by this fact alone. Arbitrary capping changes source mechanics and needs a visible approximation or omission.
- Health-percent damage is compiled from target maximum Health. Raid boss and percentage-health-resistant variants coexist in graphs, so exact source selector eligibility matters; generic enemy categories/state currently do not certify all those branches.
- Damage immunity and critical immunity are distinct from mitigation and Tenacity. Guaranteed critical attacks, true/noncritical damage and inability to critically hit need source-specific precedence.
- Execute adds target maximum Health to DPS. This is an explicit current-model proxy, not simulation of defeat, protection bypass, revive prevention, phase transitions or Galactic Legend immunity.
- Cleansing all friendly dispels as generic restoration of control output ignores which tags are actually removed, whether they can be dispelled and the recipients' actual active effects. A fully compiled DISPELL branch is not complete checked coverage.
- SELECTED, weakest/strongest and previous targets may be averaged or approximated. Selection averaging can be valid in the defined model, but requires explicit assumptions and source-verified eligibility rather than broad finite matching.

No confidence promotion follows from this audit. Corrections and explicit omission records are prerequisites for checking full kit coverage within the current model.

## Corrections implemented after the audit

Protection Up now has separate `basisStat` and Protection output pool. Source Max Health coefficients default to the receiver's Health; explicit stat queries, including Chief Nebit's own Health query, retain their own basis. The model reports `temporaryProtectionPolicy: bounded-capacity` and `temporaryProtection`, an expected active buffer bounded by application frequency and source lifetime. It adds this capacity once to EHP, honors incoming Protection bypass, and applies neither missing-Health/Protection factors nor Healing Immunity. Depletion, refresh, expiry and stacking chronology remain explicit omitted mechanics for each shield effect.

Physical and special critical avoidance, dodge and accuracy now remain separate through outgoing damage calculation. Incoming EHP uses the opponent's physical/special mixture. Vulnerable and Critical Hit Immunity sentinels now represent bounded per-channel uptime rather than enormous negative/positive avoidance stats. An attack that cannot critically hit still cannot critically hit under Vulnerable.

`DISTRIBUTE_RECOVERY` no longer appears as a fully accepted zero recovery operation. Its current-pool equalization semantics are an explicit omission. NO_KILL, outgoing SHIELD_PENETRATION, creator overrides and conditional-filter parameter semantics also receive explicit omissions rather than silently appearing complete.

`scripts/testAcceptedMechanicsAudit.mjs` independently checks Raddus's 50% shield against different recipient Health totals, Shield-versus-Health output, incoming bypass, missing-pool independence, Nebit's explicit caster-Health query, source equalization/NO_KILL gaps, physical Vulnerable's 1.5x multiplier with zero base crit chance, noncritical precedence, source physical Critical Hit Immunity and 14% physical Dodge without special-channel leakage. Arithmetic tests isolate recipients deliberately; they do not claim that every original target predicate or entire kit was certified. The separate full 341-character raw/prepared/scalar/worker checks passed after synchronization.
